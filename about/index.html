About

Terrance Luangrath

My background is in Applied Mathematics, so I tend to care about why a model works, not just whether it does.

I am finishing an M.S. in Data Science at the University of Virginia in 2026, after a B.S. in Applied Mathematics at James Madison University. Most of my week splits between research and side projects. At UVA's DART Lab I work on getting language models to triage Security Operations Center alerts, the grunt work that eats an analyst's morning, and I support other teams on human-subjects survey methodology so their results stay comparable across studies. Before UVA I spent a year at ACTFORE doing AI-assisted data mining for cyber incident response: when a company got breached, we figured out what sensitive data the attackers could reach, processing over a terabyte a day at better than ninety-nine percent accuracy under external audit.

Outside the lab my projects run wide on purpose. I have built an LLM that writes clinical risk summaries from 118,385 emergency-department encounters, a fraud pipeline that holds ninety-five percent recall at a 0.13 percent fraud rate, a shelter-detection model that reached ninety-nine percent recall on aerial imagery for earthquake relief, an agentic course recommender running on a local Llama, and a benchmark that pinned down exactly when Polars is worth the switch. Healthcare AI, audio ML, recommenders, humanitarian analytics. The range is the point.

What ties it together is that I gravitate toward problems where the metric choice matters. I optimize for recall when a missed case has real consequences, and I stay cautious about what a language model is allowed to say in a clinical setting. A model learns only what is in its input columns, and accuracy can be a trap when the thing you care about is rare, so I spend my attention on framing the problem correctly before I spend it on the model.

Right now I am looking for data science and AI engineering roles, and I am always up for a research collaboration where the questions are hard. When I am not doing any of that, I breakdance, and there is more overlap between the two than you would think. If something here caught your attention, the form below reaches me directly.
