About

Terrance Luangrath

I got into this through math, not computer science.

I finished my M.S. in Data Science at the University of Virginia in August 2026, after a B.S. in Mathematics with a minor in Data Analytics from James Madison University in 2024. Lately most of what I build runs on LLMs and agents rather than classic ML — fine-tunes, tool-calling pipelines, retrieval, the plumbing that makes a model reliable enough to hand real work to.

What I've built

  • Shipped a course-recommendation agent covering 2,759 courses, 1,754 skills, and 3,682 prerequisite relationships, built with LangChain and a local Llama 3.1, packaged in Docker Compose.
  • Built the competitive-market-insight agent for a five-agent pipeline (Ingest → Normalize → Score → Publish → Preserve) that checked metadata across 200+ entries in a 12-market by 16-technology matrix, part of a graduate capstone with Smurfit WestRock.
  • Built a fraud-detection pipeline that flags fraud across 6.3M+ transactions at 94% recall and 0.99 ROC-AUC — the hard part was the severe class imbalance. Deployed on Azure ML and Azure Container Apps.
  • Benchmarked a CNN against an Audio Spectrogram Transformer for keyword spotting under noise in PyTorch. The CNN won on clean audio, 94.76% to 90.52%, and stayed ahead as the noise got worse.
  • Validated 1,500+ records a day for a year as a Junior Data Analyst at ACTFORE, keeping data accuracy above 99%, June 2024 to May 2025.

What I want next is AI engineering work — building the systems around LLMs, not just the models themselves, and figuring out how you verify one got something right before anyone downstream trusts it.
