Associate AI Engineer for Data Scientists

OCTOBER 15, 2026 · Career · Machine Learning

I finished DataCamp's Associate AI Engineer for Data Scientists track, which started with Supervised Learning with scikit-learn and ended with Introduction to Testing in Python.

What I thought of it

A lot of the track reviewed what I learned in my master's program, like supervised and unsupervised learning and deep learning with PyTorch. The newer material was the software engineering foundation: packages and classes, Git, and testing. MLOps Concepts sat somewhere in between, and Working with Llama 3 deepened my understanding of LLMs.

The track was fun and worth doing.

The new role

I'm starting a new role as an AI Agent & Workflow Developer, and I hope what I learned here applies to it.

What's next

The AI Engineer for Data Scientists Associate Certification. It has a timed exam and a practical exam.
