I finished DataCamp's [Associate AI Engineer for Data Scientists](https://www.datacamp.com/completed/statement-of-accomplishment/track/3350a3c3bb81b016e1ecf31badc127147603fd24) track, which started with [Supervised Learning with scikit-learn](/journal/refreshing-supervised-learning-with-scikit-learn/) and ended with [Introduction to Testing in Python](/journal/introduction-to-testing-in-python/).

![Associate AI Engineer for Data Scientists track completion badge](/assets/img/datacamp_associate_ai_engineer_track_badge.png)

## What I thought of it

A lot of the track reviewed what I learned in my master's program, like [supervised and unsupervised learning](/journal/practicing-unsupervised-learning-in-python/) and [deep learning with PyTorch](/journal/introduction-to-deep-learning-with-pytorch/). The newer material was the software engineering foundation: [packages and classes](/journal/software-engineering-principles-in-python/), [Git](/journal/introduction-to-git/), and [testing](/journal/introduction-to-testing-in-python/). [MLOps Concepts](/journal/mlops-concepts/) sat somewhere in between, and [Working with Llama 3](/journal/working-with-llama-3/) deepened my understanding of LLMs.

The track was fun and worth doing.

## The new role

I'm starting a new role as an AI Agent & Workflow Developer, and I hope what I learned here applies to it.

## What's next

The [AI Engineer for Data Scientists Associate Certification](https://www.datacamp.com/). It has a timed exam and a practical exam.
